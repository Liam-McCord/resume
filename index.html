About Me

My Drive

I'm a fourth-year Mechatronics Engineering student with a strong curiosity about how the universe works and a constant drive to learn. That curiosity has led me into a wide range of engineering projects, but I've found myself especially drawn to aerospace. It presents some of the most challenging and rewarding problems I've come across.

Along the way, I've built hands-on experience through both personal and academic projects, as well as time spent at two aerospace companies. I enjoy getting stuck into complex problems and figuring out how things work, whether that's designing, building, or testing.

I'm persistent by nature and tend to approach challenges from multiple angles until something clicks. I'm comfortable working independently, but I also enjoy being part of a team and learning from others. Ultimately, I'm motivated by working on meaningful, cutting-edge projects and contributing to something bigger than myself.

Outside of Engineering

My hobbies include 3D animation, biking, guitar, piano, collecting old books, and astronomy. I love going out at night and looking at the stars.
